Native Image vs JVM Comparison

Native Image

  • Startup: 10-100ms
  • Memory: 30-80MB
  • No JVM required
  • Serverless-ready
  • Instant scaling
  • Smaller attack surface

Traditional JVM

  • Startup: 2-10 seconds
  • Memory: 200-500MB
  • JVM required
  • Warm-up needed
  • Slower scaling
  • Larger footprint
